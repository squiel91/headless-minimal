# Seed images

18 images in 9 sessions. Save each as `<name>.jpg`, under 300 KB.

**How to generate:** open one new conversation per session and send its
prompts in order, waiting for each image before sending the next. The first
prompt of a session is complete on its own; the following ones continue from
the previous image, so the product, light and framing stay consistent.

## Art direction (already included in every prompt)

- **Background:** warm greige limewash plaster wall (sandy taupe, around
  `#CBBFAE`) with a subtle mottled texture. Never white: the store pages are
  white, so the photos must read as warm blocks of color against them.
- **Surface:** pale natural oak tabletop for objects; warm gray microcement
  floor for furniture.
- **Light:** soft diffused daylight from a large window on the left, gentle
  shadows falling to the right, no harsh highlights.
- **Camera:** eye level, slightly above the subject (about 15°), 50 mm lens,
  product centered with generous empty space around it.
- **Palette and grading:** sand, oat, stone, clay and charcoal; low
  saturation, soft contrast, matte editorial look, subtle film grain.
- **Never:** text, logos, people, hands, extra props, white backgrounds.
